# Python 3.8 → 3.12 Sprint Plan (40 services, 2 weeks)

## The one change I'd make to the director's plan

Keep the freeze and the two-week deadline. Replace the single big-bang Friday deploy with a staging cut-over at the end of week 1 and production waves on Tue–Thu of week 2. Nothing deploys on the last Friday.

Here is why, using your own constraint.

- **Rollback.** A single Friday deploy of 40 services means 40 simultaneous failure modes going into a weekend on-call. You can't tell which change caused a failure, and you can't roll back one service without touching the rest.
- **The shared chart.** The base image is pinned in one `values.yaml`, so a naive change flips all 40 services at once. That is the real big-bang risk. Fix it before anything else (Day 1).
- **Same deadline.** Everyone still upgrades during the sprint. Only the production flip is staggered.

The director gets "all 40 on 3.12 within the sprint." On-call gets a rollback that works per service.

## Design

**Problem:** Python 3.8 is end-of-life, so we get no security patches.

**Success criteria (measurable):**
1. All 40 services run 3.12 in production by Thu of week 2. Any exceptions are named, with an owner and a date.
2. Per service, error rate and p95 latency stay within an agreed margin of the pre-deploy baseline for 24h.
3. A rollback was rehearsed in staging and works for every service.
4. The 3.8 image and the per-service overrides are gone by sprint end.

**Out of scope:** new features (frozen), dependency major-version upgrades beyond what 3.12 forces, and Python 3.13. Hotfixes are allowed and go through the same pipeline.

**Single source of truth for the Python version.** Put the default image tag in the chart's `values.yaml` and let CI read it from there, so the version isn't copied into CI configs. Add a per-service override only for services not yet migrated. These overrides are temporary and are deleted on Day 10. Don't paste `3.12` into 40 places.

**Rollback invariant.** Every change merged before the image flip must run on both 3.8 and 3.12:
- No 3.9+ syntax or typing features until the migration is complete.
- CI runs a 3.8 and 3.12 matrix.
- Rolling back is then just a Helm rollback or an override to the previous image. The 3.8 image and the cp38 wheels stay available until the cleanup step.

## Work tracks

| Track | Services | Work | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: Standard | 31 | Recompile lockfiles, fix 3.12 breakages, green CI on the matrix | Low. Coverage is 60–85%, so confirm the "pure-Python deps" claim with a Day 1 install smoke test. |
| B: asynctest | 4 | Migrate to `unittest.IsolatedAsyncioTestCase` or `pytest-asyncio`. This is mechanical and the tests must pass on 3.8 as well. | Low |
| C: libgeo-py | 2 | Build and publish cp312 wheels from CI, and check the C code against 3.12 (removed `Py_UNICODE` APIs, etc.). Needs a named owner today. | **Critical path.** |
| D: distutils | 1 | Move `setup.py` to setuptools or `pyproject.toml`. Note that 3.12 venvs no longer bundle setuptools. | Low |
| E: No tests | 2 | Write characterization tests first, then upgrade. For the payments webhook: recorded real payloads, signature verification, idempotency or retry behavior, and error paths. | **Highest business risk.** |

Common 3.12 breakages to grep for on Day 1:
- `imp`, `asyncore`, `asynchat` and `smtpd` are removed.
- `assertEquals` and the other unittest aliases are removed.
- `collections.Mapping` and similar ABC aliases are removed.
- Binary wheels for DB drivers and crypto libraries need 3.12 builds.

Run CI with `-W error::DeprecationWarning` to surface problems early.

## Schedule

**Week 1: build**
- **Day 1 (Mon).**
  - Kickoff and ownership: one owner per track, and a named owner for libgeo-py.
  - Chart change: per-service image override with 3.12 as the default.
  - Build and publish the 3.12 base image.
  - Add the CI matrix template.
  - Run a 3.12 install and test smoke across all 40 and triage the results.
  - Start libgeo-py wheel work and the payments characterization tests immediately.
- **Days 2–4.** Tracks A, B, D and E in parallel. Each service merges its dual-compat PR as soon as it is green on both versions. Assign services to engineers by team, with no more than about 4 each.
- **Day 3 checkpoint.**
  - Is libgeo-py on track? If cp312 wheels aren't building, escalate now.
  - Fallback: those 2 services stay on 3.8 via override and ship as a documented exception. They don't block the other 38.
- **Day 5 (Fri).**
  - Flip all of staging to 3.12.
  - Run a rollback rehearsal on every service, and write down the exact commands in a runbook.
  - Freeze merges except fixes.

**Week 2: ship**
- **Day 6 (Mon).** Staging soak with synthetic traffic and fixes. Go/no-go meeting at end of day with the on-call lead. Confirm baselines and alert thresholds for each service.
- **Day 7 (Tue), wave 1.** The 6 lowest-traffic services from Track A. These are the canary for the process. Watch for at least 4h before wave 2.
- **Day 8 (Wed), wave 2.** The remaining Track A services, the untested non-payments service, and the 4 asynctest services.
- **Day 9 (Thu), wave 3.**
  - Morning: the distutils service and the 2 libgeo-py services (if the wheels are ready).
  - Then the payments webhook receiver, alone, during business hours with on-call watching. Use a canary or partial rollout, and confirm the payment provider's webhook retry behavior covers any dropped events during the rollout.
- **Day 10 (Fri).**
  - No deploys.
  - Soak and monitor.
  - Cleanup: delete the overrides and the 3.8 image reference, drop the 3.8 half of the CI matrix, and update the runbook.
  - Retro.

## Rollback

- **Per service:** `helm rollback` or set the override back to the 3.8 image. This works because of the dual-compat invariant.
- **Trigger:** an error-rate or latency breach over the agreed margin, for the agreed duration, per service. Define the numbers on Day 6.
- **Cleanup only after the 24h soak:** don't remove 3.8 compatibility or the image until every service has soaked.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| libgeo-py wheels slip | Day 3 checkpoint; exception path leaves those 2 on 3.8 |
| Payments service has no safety net | Characterization tests are a gating item; deploy it last and alone |
| A hidden transitive dependency has no 3.12 wheel | Day 1 install smoke across all 40 |
| Freeze leaks (hotfixes drift) | Hotfixes go through the same dual-compat CI matrix |
| Staging doesn't reflect production | Traffic replay or synthetic load for the 6 canary services |
